Plan:
- tempo 80 (1 beat = 0.75 s), time 2/4. turnout { out(80) } from the start, steps first word
  `together` (turned out: heels touch = first position). height { straight } as the ballet base.
  arms bras bas = the grammar's example `fwd.across(30).down(70).bent(40).elbow(side)`, held.
- Timeline: beat 0 standing; beats 0-1 down into the prep plie (height low(8cm) at beat 1);
  sautes land at beats 2,3,4,5 as `jump.up(10cm)` (the written height makes them equal; 10 cm

Hesitations:
- The grammar says "the plie before a jump is not written" and a height plie on the way in

  (it says it does) -> the sheet will tell.
- How to go from first into fifth: no jump word lands "in fifth"; I use a step + shift.center.
  An alternative was a leg gesture with skip. Not a jump into fifth (the task did not say).
- "1 拍立つ" at the end: straight at 11, held to 12.

Expected diagnostics: maybe SH2016 on the changements (15 cm needs ~0.69 s, a beat is 0.75 s,
but the step/shift before the first changement is short: shift.center is 1 beat, ok).
Maybe something on the step from both feet (rightFoot step while weight is on both).

Predictions:
(a) saute ankle peak: ankle rest height ~8 cm + 10 cm rise = ~18 cm, the same for all 4
    (18, 18, 18, 18). Possibly a little less if the takeoff is from low.
(b) after the 4th changement: right foot front (R->L->R->L->R).

assemble.shosa — BEFORE
=====================================================================
Reading of the task: it says "右、左、右、左" but also "後ろにある足" (the foot that is behind).
Starting in fifth right front, the foot behind is the LEFT. Dessus = the working (back) foot
lands in front. So the working feet are L, R, L, R (landing fronts L, R, L, R). I follow the
"back foot" rule, since the task insists on it ("ここでは後ろにある足").
Plan:
- tempo 96 (0.625 s a beat), 2/4. turnout out(80), height straight, steps first word
  `rightFoot.fwd(0.08m).across(0.13m)` (fifth, on both feet).
- Bar 1 [0,2) is the preparation (the first assemble needs a brush before it; beat 0 cannot hold a
  movement). Assembles in bars 2-5: take off near each bar's head, land on its 2nd beat: beats
  3, 5, 7, 9. Bar 6: stand in fifth, arms en bas.
- The landing in fifth with the working foot in front: `jump.change.up(12cm)` — "lands with the
  feet as they stood, mirrored". The steps row still thinks the feet stand in fifth, so the change
  puts the working foot (the back one) in front. The working leg's row brushes it out and then
  `skip` hands it back to steps for the landing (the grammar's "skip lets the leg go back to steps").
- Leg rows: leftLeg { skip:1 then:1 | side.down(45):1/2 skip:3/2 | skip:1 then:1 | side.down(45):1/2 skip:3/2 | skip:2 | skip:2 | }
  — lifts in beat 1-2 (then:1), at 45° at beat 2 (bar head), held to 2.5, then skip during the
  flight, the foot joins the other in the air and lands at 3. Right leg the same, one bar later.
- Arms: basArms -> second (`fwd.side(70).down(15).bent(20)`) at the bar head (take-off), back to
  bas at the landing; repeated.
Hesitations:
- Is a jump from one foot with jump.change allowed (change is defined from "how the feet stood")?
  The table says a jump from one foot is an assemble, but does not say where the free foot lands.
- "Brush along the floor" (擦って): there is no word for a foot sliding out along the floor into a
  gesture (a battement tendu/glissé). A leg gesture lifts; I accept that it lifts off.
- The leg lift during then:1 happens while the previous jump has just landed (right after the
  landing at 3 the right leg lifts in 3-4). Maybe SH2004 if the landing counts as "in a step".
- The brush 45° = `side.down(45)`.
Expected diagnostics: maybe SH2004 (leg gesture right after a landing), maybe SH2016 (12 cm needs
  ~0.55 s; a beat is 0.625 s, ok), maybe SH2013 if the skip does not put the foot down.
Predictions:
(a) after each assemble the front foot: L, R, L, R.
(b) assemble 1, the working (left) ankle's highest point above the floor: ~33 cm.

Summary of grammar issues (for the report)
=====================================================================
1. Prep plie before a jump: the task asks for one; the grammar says don't write it (the jump bends
   itself) and a written one makes a double bob. There is no way to say "take off from the plie I
   am standing in" (e.g. a jump whose bend is the height row's). Got: 8 cm plie, then down to 20 cm
   below standing before take-off.
2. Skip spanning a jump landing -> SH2013; skip ending exactly at the landing -> ok. The grammar's
   sentence "a step of that foot in that time carries it there" suggests any skip covering the step
   works. And SH2013's message suggests only `down` (which would put the foot back where it stood).
   Improve: "the skip must END on the beat of the step/landing", and mention `skip` in SH2013.
3. jump.change from one foot (assemble dessus) worked, but the table defines `change` only for a
   jump from both feet (changement) and calls a jump from one foot "an assemble" with no word for
   where the free foot lands (front/back). Add an example: assemble dessus/dessous =
   leg row brush + skip ending at the landing + jump.change / plain jump.
4. No second-position arms in the examples (bras bas, first, fifth are given).
5. No brush along the floor (tendu / glisse) before a gesture.
6. A foot closing straight passes through the standing ankle (both in front and behind closes):
   no warning; grammar says "close by".
7. Changing first -> fifth between jumps: no hint; step + shift.center worked.
