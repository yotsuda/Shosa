GENERAL NOTES (things I could not say / grammar text)
- Unclear before trying whether a mark may sit on `then` (it can: worked for "the moment the spring starts").
  The grammar says "Any event that moves something can carry one"; `then` is a wait, so this was a guess.
- Unclear whether `jump.fwd.apart` combines (it does); the jump-word table lists them separately.
- `@linear` on the low word: grammar says "for one unbroken movement, @linear on that word and on the one after";
  I only wanted the way TO it constant, which worked. But corners are rounded ~0.07 s by smoothing, and the
  score's frame 0 is already 6 mm into the sink; the 0.2 s spring is really ~0.3 s with soft edges and starts one
  frame before its mark. Not documented in the grammar.
- `bent(0)` on `down` silently = default 10 deg; the elbow never gets below 5 deg. Grammar says "0 straight".
- No way to give a standing knee's angle; only the hip drop (low(Ncm)); had to search by trial (0.7cm ~ 15 deg).
- Sigh: no chest/breath word; only hip height.
- I used [IO.File]::WriteAllText once in scratch for a test file (tool convention slip, scratch only).
