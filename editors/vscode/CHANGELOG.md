# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Hand in hand**: an arm can take another dancer's hand by its side — `a { leftArm { b.rightHand.low } }` with `b { rightArm { a.leftHand.low } }` is two side by side holding their inner hands (until now only hands of the same side met: a handshake), and two who face take both hands straight across. `neighbor` in place of a role is whoever stands next to the dancer on that arm's side, so one role danced by a whole line says it: `arms { neighbor.hand:2 then:6 }` joins the line, `neighbor.hand.high` lifts the joined hands, and the two ends hold their free hand the same way. The hands stay joined while the dancers walk, sway and bow, and a lifted hand turns to the one it holds by itself, palm to palm. In the preview the characters' own hands are brought together (a child beside a grown-up holds hands too). `shosa:idol` has `curtainCall` (hands taken, lifted, a bow under them), and `samples/holding-hands.shosa` shows it. Two who let go of each other at once (a handshake, a high five) now leave the place where they met: each used to follow the other's hand on its way down.

- **An idol's vocabulary**: `using "shosa:idol"` brings in the gestures an audience reads at once, by name — poses (`peaceEye`, `heartHands`, `cheekPoint`, `catPaws`, `saluteWink`, `wish`, `earListen`, `handOnHeart`, `callOut`, `fistUp`, `idolFinish`) and moves (`twoHandWave`, `blowKiss`, `beckon`, `twinkle`, `pointAround`, `rainbowArc`, `clapUp`, `cuteSway`, `armSwing`, `fistPump`, `heartBeat`, `bunnyHop`, `starJump`, `idolBow`, `idolTurn` and two combinations). They write the fingers and the face with the arms, and lie over the feet of `shosa:basics`. The choreography guide lists them, so an assistant asked for an idol dance puts them on the words. `samples/idol.shosa` shows them.

- **Formations**: `formation vee { alice at 0 0.8  bob at -1 -0.4  carol at 1 -0.4 }` says where each dancer stands, and one word of `steps`, `go.vee`, walks each dancer of a role to its own place, standing there on the beat (`go.home`: back to its `at`). The steps are made for it: a place near by is walked facing the front (forward, back, or a grapevine sideways), a place far away is walked as people walk — the body turns the way it goes, walks there, and turns back. Dancers whose ways cross go round each other by themselves, or as written (`go.vee.front`, `go.vee.back`: passing on the audience's side or upstage); `steps(8)` says how many steps. A walk too far for its time is said (SH2026). Completion and hover know the formations. `samples/formation.shosa` shows it.

- **The face**: a `face` row gives a character a look and its eyes — `face { smile(50):4 | smile(60).winkRight:1 smile(50):3 | }` — with `smile`, `sad`, `angry`, `relaxed`, `surprised`, `neutral`, and `closed`, `winkLeft`, `winkRight`; a bracket is how much, in percent. It is there on its beat and changes in a moment. The eyes blink by themselves, written or not. The preview and its MP4 show it on a VRM character (a wink on top of a smile shuts the eye no further than the smile leaves of it, measured on each character), and the `.vrma` export keys VRM's expressions. The row's name is coloured as the other rows' are. `samples/face.shosa` shows it.

- **Fixed**: an arm sent between two places nearly opposite from the shoulder (from the belly of a bow to a hand held high, while the body rises) could jump from one way round to the other in a single frame, and `check` said "too fast" (SH2003) of a word that had time enough. The arm now goes one way.

- **Fixed**: a character whose legs are shorter or longer than the figure's stood somewhere else than the dance put it once it had walked — short of its place in a formation, and too far from the hand it was to hold. A character now stands where the figure stands; its steps are still its own length where it dances in one place. And two characters of different heights who lift their joined hands meet where both arms reach: the taller one's hand comes down to the smaller one's. A character's own two hands meet where the figure's do (a clap, a heart), whatever the length of its arms; `clapUp` of `shosa:idol` claps (its hands stopped 12 cm apart).

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-27

The first release, for Windows (x64): **Shosa 3D Choreographer** in the Extensions view and the Marketplace; its commands, settings and files are `shosa`. What a dance does with a song's words (the mouth, `shosa song`'s words, Add a Dance… placing moves on them) and a silent recording need the Lily# extension 0.18.0 or later.

### Writing

- The Shosa language for `.shosa` files: diagnostics, completion and hover for every word, from the same table the compiler checks against.
- **File > New File... > Shosa File** starts from a file that already compiles.
- A problem with a word of a `move` or a `pose` is shown once, at that word inside it, with the beats where it happens, not at every place the move is used.
- **Sitting**: `height { sit(0.45m) }` sits down on a seat that high (a chair, a sofa, a stool), the feet on the floor, and any other height stands up again. Seated, the arms, the torso and the head say what they say standing.
- **Juggling**: `dancer … juggles balls` (or `clubs`, `rings`) and a `juggle` row in siteswap as jugglers write it: `juggle { every 1/2  3:4 | 531531 3 3 | }`. A pattern that cannot be juggled, or cannot follow the one before it, is said where it goes wrong. Tricks go on a pattern with `@`: `3:4@mills`, `@windmill`, `@tennis`, `@halfShower`, `423:4@takeouts`, catches behind the back and throws under the leg. The legs, the torso and the head dance their own rows meanwhile.
- **Hands**: a row for the hands (`hands`, `leftHand`, `rightHand`) says the shape of the fingers — `fist`, `open`, `spread`, `point`, `peace`, `thumbUp`, `heart`, `count(3)` and more, or finger by finger (`fist.index(0)`) — and which way the fingers point and the palm faces (`open.fingers(up).palm(fwd)`). A character shows them, and the export of a dance that writes them carries the finger bones.
- **A passage that flows, or hits**: a `quality` row says once how the parts go to their words — `quality { through }` passes every word without stopping (one flowing line), `quality { sharp:4 smooth:4 }` hits the next four beats and then eases again — so the marks need not be written on every word. A word's own `@` wins. And a word passed through (`@through`) is passed on one curve: the hand no longer turns a corner at it.
- **Off the feet**: the weight can go onto the hands, the head, the back, the front, a seat on the floor or the knees (`steps { hands.fwd(0.6m) }`, `{ head }`, `{ back }`, `{ seat }`, `{ knees }`, and `{ feet }` to stand again), and the torso's row then lays the whole body out: a handstand, a plank, a headspin, a backspin, sitting, kneeling, lying down. The legs are free there (`legs { side.up(40) }`), and a hand goes to the floor with `side.floor`.
- **More of ballet**: a jump that lands in any stance (`jump.rightFoot.fwd(0.08m).across(0.13m)` closes into fifth), beats (`jump.beat`, `jump.change.beat(2)`), a foot `pointed` or `flexed` in the air, `height { pointe }`, a toe at the shin or the thigh, a toe resting on the floor with the knee bent, a hand on the thigh or the knee.
- **Shosa's own vocabulary** (`using "shosa:basics"`, `"shosa:pop"`, `"shosa:jazz"`): poses and moves ready to dance by name. Two more are **experimental** — `"shosa:ballet"` and `"shosa:break"`: ballet reads as a beginner at the barre, breaking has the shapes without the momentum; dancers' corrections are welcome.
- Parts of the body you do not write are not frozen: breathing, the weight shifting from foot to foot, the trunk and head following the arms, and the arms swinging with a walk.

### Preview

- **Open Preview to the Side** (Ctrl+K V): the figures move as you type. The word being performed is lit in the source; put the cursor on a word to see its pose; click a limb to go to the word that put it there. An edit of a long dance does not hold the preview up: only the frames it moved are sent and worked out again.
- **Dancing to a song**: `music "song.lys"` takes the tempo, the time and the order of the sections from a [Lily#](https://yotsuda.github.io/LilySharp/) song, and the preview plays the song with the dance, through the sound font Lily# plays with (**Lily#: Choose Sound Font…**; one setting for both). The song is read by the Lily# extension, which is needed only for this.
- A score without a song is heard as a click on every beat and a clap at every mark (`!hit`).
- **Camera** (the button at the bottom of the preview): **Fit** (or F) frames the whole score, every dancer and everything they juggle from start to end, as close as it fits; **Zoom**, **Height** and **Angle** move the view by hand, and **Reset** puts it back. The view is kept, and the MP4 is filmed from it.
- *Preview:* **A VRM character for a dancer**: `dancer alice humanoid "girl.vrm" on lead` dresses the dancer in a VRM model in the preview and its MP4. The export of the movement does not change. `height 1.5m` draws the character that tall, to the top of its hair, so models made at different sizes stand side by side as you want them. A character's arms are kept out of its own body, which is not the shape the movement was solved on: the elbow finds its way round first, so a hand stays where the words put it. A hand a word puts at the head (`head`, `chin`, `forehead`) is put there on the character's own head, measured from its mesh, so it is not lost in a big head or its hair. Set `shosa.models.folder` to the folder of your models, and with the cursor on the file of a dancer line, Ctrl+Shift+Up/Down switch to the model before or after it. A dancer's file is looked for in the dance's own folder first, then in that folder, and the keys write it from that folder (`"girl.vrm"`).
- *Preview:* **Singing**: `dancer alice humanoid "girl.vrm" sings melody on lead` moves the character's mouth to the words that part of the Lily# song sings, and the `.vrma` export carries it. `mic right` puts a mic in the hand: it is at the mouth while the dancer sings, and dances with the arm between phrases.
- *Preview:* **Dancing to a recording**: when a song made from a recording has its `NAME.orecchiante.json` beside it, the dance keeps the recording's beat, and the preview and the MP4 play the recording. A recording that Lily# marks silent (`"silent": true`, after **Arrange an Accompaniment…**) still keeps the beat but is not played: the song's parts are heard instead.
- *Preview:* **Mixer**: with the Lily# extension 0.17.0 or later, which hands out a song one part at a time, the preview plays each part of the score, each singing voice and the recording (or its separated parts) at the level you give it, and the MP4 has the same mix.

### Export

- **Model**: glTF (`.glb`), VRM Animation (`.vrma`) or BVH (`.bvh`), with the marks for a game engine.
- **MP4**: a video of the preview with its sound, encoded by the editor itself (no ffmpeg). The shape is the view's, 16:9 (1920×1080) or 9:16 (1080×1920, for YouTube Shorts); a fixed shape is framed in the preview.
- *Preview:* **Audio**: the song as the Mixer has it, for as long as the dance, as an MP3 or a WAV (written by the Lily# extension).

### For AI assistants

- **A vocabulary of poses and moves by style**, built in: `using "shosa:basics"`, `"shosa:pop"` (80s pop: hits, kicks, spins, toe stands, robot), `"shosa:jazz"` and (experimental) `"shosa:ballet"` bring in more than a hundred named poses and moves, each saying what it is and where it fits, to dance with by name.
- **A choreography guide** for AI assistants (`#shosaGuide`, `shosa guide`): plan the dance, write it a section at a time, dance a song's repeated sections from one move each, keep it varied, and choose from the vocabulary.
- Copilot Chat's agent mode gets six tools: the language (`#shosaGrammar`), the choreography guide (`#shosaGuide`), a way to write a dance that stops for no confirmation (`#shosaEdit`), the compiler's verdict (`#shosaCheck`), a picture of the movement (`#shosaSheet`) and the song a dance is written to (`#shosaSong`: its sections with their bars and seconds, who sings, and the words bar by bar on their beats). `shosa song SONG.lys` prints the same in a terminal.
- **Add a Dance…** on a Lily# song (right-click a `.lys`): a dance file begun beside it with a dancer for each part that sings, opened with the preview, and Copilot's chat asked to choreograph it to the words.
- The compiler `shosa` comes with the extension and is on the PATH of VS Code's terminals (`shosa grammar` prints the whole language for an agent).
